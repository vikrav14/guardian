# Atmospheric weather artwork

`weather_atlas.webp` is a losslessly encoded RGBA sprite atlas generated with the built-in image-generation tool for Guardian's approved atmospheric design. It is bundled locally; rendering it makes no external image request. The original generated artwork was PNG; WebP preserves the displayed pixels and alpha at a smaller download size.

The 1254 × 1254 atlas has nine equal 418 × 418 cells:

| Row | Left | Centre | Right |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0 | Clear day | Partly cloudy day | Cloudy |
| 1 | Rain | Thunderstorm | Wind |
| 2 | Clear night | Mist | Snow |

Generation brief: a square transparent 3×3 production weather atlas matching Guardian's atmospheric concept; premium softly sculpted ivory/pearl 3D clouds, a golden sun and crescent moon, delicate blue rain, a restrained golden lightning bolt, flowing aqua wind ribbons, fog and snowflakes. Consistent scale and lighting, individually centred illustrations, no labels, UI, ground plane or painted background. The approved six-condition concept was the visual reference.

Condition text remains available independently of artwork. Unknown or unavailable data does not select a weather illustration.
